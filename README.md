## 🌷 What is Lumi?

**Lumi Wellness** is a digital wellness platform designed to help users build healthier routines through **habit tracking, personal reflection, progress analytics and an AI-ready companion experience.**

Instead of simply collecting wellness data, Lumi is designed around a more human question:

### 💭 "What can my daily actions teach me about myself?"

The platform brings together:

🌸 Habits  
💧 Hydration  
😴 Sleep  
🏃 Exercise  
💜 Mood  
📖 Journaling  
📊 Progress  
🔥 Streaks  
🏆 Achievements  
🌱 Companion growth  
🤖 AI personalization

---

## ✨ Why I Built Lumi

Many wellness applications focus on numbers:

> "You completed 3 habits."

Lumi explores something different:

> **"You are building consistency."**

The product combines **data visualization, gamification and personalization** to transform small daily actions into a more engaging experience.

The long-term vision is to evolve Lumi from a wellness tracker into an **AI-powered personal companion** capable of understanding patterns and providing contextual recommendations.
