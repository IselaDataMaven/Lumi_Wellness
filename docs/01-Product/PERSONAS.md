# 1. Purpose

This document defines the primary user personas for Lumi Wellness.

Personas help the team understand:

* Who we design for
* What users need
* What challenges they experience
* How Lumi can provide meaningful support

---

# 2. Persona Philosophy

Personas are not stereotypes.

They represent patterns of needs, behaviors, and experiences.

Every design decision should consider:

🌿 Human needs

🤍 Emotional context

✨ Accessibility

🌸 Personal dignity

---

# 3. Primary Persona

# 🌸 Persona 01 — The Wellness Seeker

## Overview

A person looking for gentle support to better understand and care for their daily wellbeing.

They want tools that help them create awareness without feeling judged.

---

## Goals

They want to:

* Understand their daily patterns
* Build healthier routines
* Track how they feel
* Feel supported during difficult days

---

## Challenges

They may experience:

* Difficulty maintaining routines
* Feeling overwhelmed by complex apps
* Lack of personalized support
* Frustration with rigid wellness expectations

---

## Needs From Lumi

Lumi should provide:

* Simple interactions
* Compassionate language
* Flexible routines
* Encouragement without pressure

---

## Behaviors

They prefer:

* Short activities
* Clear guidance
* Calm interfaces
* Personal reflection

---

# 4. Primary Persona

# 🌿 Persona 02 — The Chronic Condition Navigator

## Overview

A person living with ongoing health challenges who wants better self-awareness and daily support.

Lumi Wellness should complement professional care, not replace it.

---

## Goals

They want to:

* Understand their daily experience
* Identify patterns
* Manage routines
* Feel more in control

---

## Challenges

They may experience:

* Variable energy levels
* Difficulty maintaining habits
* Emotional fatigue
* Information overload

---

## Needs From Lumi

Lumi should offer:

* Flexible experiences
* Respect for changing energy levels
* Non-judgmental feedback
* Simple tracking

---

## Behaviors

They value:

* Personalization
* Accessibility
* Privacy
* Gentle reminders

---

# 5. Secondary Persona

# 🌸 Persona 03 — The Supportive Companion

## Overview

A person who wants to better support someone they care about.

---

## Goals

They want to:

* Understand experiences better
* Provide emotional support
* Learn how to help

---

## Challenges

They may experience:

* Uncertainty about what support is helpful
* Lack of understanding
* Communication difficulties

---

## Needs From Lumi

Lumi may provide:

* Educational resources
* Communication guidance
* Supportive information

---

# 6. User Characteristics

Lumi users may vary in:

* Age
* Technology experience
* Accessibility needs
* Daily routines
* Wellness goals

The product should adapt instead of requiring users to adapt.

---

# 7. User Frustrations

Common frustrations to consider:

* Complex interfaces
* Too many notifications
* Judgmental language
* Excessive tracking
* Feeling like they failed

---

# 8. User Motivations

Users may be motivated by:

* Understanding themselves better
* Creating sustainable habits
* Feeling supported
* Improving daily quality of life

---

# 9. Accessibility Considerations

Personas should include users with different needs.

Consider:

* Visual accessibility
* Motor accessibility
* Cognitive load
* Fatigue limitations
* Different technology abilities

---

# 10. Design Implications

Personas influence:

## UX

Create calm and predictable experiences.

---

## Content

Use supportive communication.

---

## AI

Provide helpful guidance with transparency.

---

## Features

Prioritize meaningful value over unnecessary complexity.

---

# 11. Persona Validation

Personas should evolve through:

* User research
* Feedback
* Interviews
* Product analytics

They should never replace listening to real users.

---

# Final Principle

Lumi Wellness is built for people, not metrics.

Every persona represents a human experience that deserves empathy, respect, and thoughtful design.
