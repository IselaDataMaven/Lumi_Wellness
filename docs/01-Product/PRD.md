# 1. Executive Summary

Lumi Wellness is a modern wellness application designed to support people living with Fibromyalgia, chronic pain, anxiety, fatigue, and stress through gentle movement, emotional tracking, and personalized wellness routines.

Rather than functioning as a medical application, Lumi Wellness acts as a compassionate digital companion that encourages healthy habits while respecting the physical and emotional limitations of its users.

The application combines evidence-based wellness practices with a calming and accessible user experience to reduce cognitive load and create a safe, welcoming environment.

---

# 2. Vision

To become the most empathetic digital wellness companion for people living with chronic pain by making self-care simple, accessible, and emotionally supportive.

---

# 3. Mission

Help users improve their quality of life through gentle daily movement, emotional awareness, and positive habit formation without creating pressure or guilt.

---

# 4. Problem Statement

Millions of people living with Fibromyalgia and chronic pain struggle to maintain healthy movement routines due to pain, fatigue, anxiety, and inconsistent motivation.

Most existing fitness applications focus on performance, competition, or calorie tracking, which often creates frustration rather than encouragement.

Users need a product that understands fluctuating energy levels and celebrates consistency instead of perfection.

# 18. Definition of Success

Lumi Wellness succeeds when users feel emotionally supported and empowered to care for themselves consistently, regardless of the number of exercises completed.

Success is measured by improved engagement, sustained healthy habits, and a positive user experience rather than physical performance alone.

---

# 19. Product Principles

Kindness over pressure.

Consistency over perfection.

Accessibility by default.

Privacy by design.

Simple is powerful.

Small steps matter.

Every user deserves compassion.

---

20. Product Design Principles

Human-first: Every decision should reduce the user’s physical or mental burden.

Calm Technology: The app supports the user, not interrupts them.

Accessibility by Default: All new features must be accessible from their very first version.

Privacy by Design: User data belongs to the user.

Evidence-Informed: Well-being recommendations should be based on recognized best practices, avoiding unsupported medical claims.

Progress, not Perfection: The goal is to create sustainable habits, not to maximize metrics.
