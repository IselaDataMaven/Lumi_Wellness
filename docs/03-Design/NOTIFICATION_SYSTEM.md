# 1. Purpose

This document defines how notifications should be designed and used across Lumi Wellness.

Notifications help users stay connected with their wellness journey while respecting their time, energy, and personal preferences.

The goal is to create notifications that feel:

🌿 Helpful

🤍 Respectful

✨ Timely

🌸 Supportive

---

# 2. Notification Philosophy

Notifications are invitations, not obligations.

Lumi should never create pressure or guilt.

The purpose is not:

"Make users open the app."

The purpose is:

"Offer support when it may be useful."

---

# 3. Notification Principles

## Respect the user

Users control:

* Frequency
* Categories
* Timing
* Permission settings

---

## Add value

Every notification should answer:

"Why does this matter to the user?"

If there is no clear benefit, do not send it.

---

## Be emotionally aware

Consider:

* User energy
* Context
* Previous interactions
* Personal preferences

---

## Avoid guilt

Never communicate failure.

Avoid:

"You missed your activity."

Prefer:

"Your wellness moment is here whenever you feel ready."

---

# 4. Notification Types

## Wellness Reminders

Purpose:

Support routines and self-care moments.

Examples:

"Your reflection time is available whenever you are ready."

---

## Activity Reminders

Purpose:

Encourage optional activities.

Examples:

"Would a short breathing exercise feel helpful today?"

---

## Journal Reminders

Purpose:

Encourage reflection.

Examples:

"Take a moment to check in with yourself."

---

## Progress Notifications

Purpose:

Celebrate meaningful progress.

Examples:

"You've created five moments of reflection this month."

---

## AI Notifications

Purpose:

Inform users about Lumi AI experiences.

Examples:

"Your personalized wellness summary is ready."

---

## System Notifications

Purpose:

Communicate important product information.

Examples:

* Account updates
* Security alerts
* Feature changes

---

# 5. Notification Timing

Notifications should consider:

* User preferences
* Time zones
* Activity patterns
* Quiet hours

Avoid:

* Early morning interruptions
* Late night messages
* Excessive frequency

---

# 6. Frequency Guidelines

Quality over quantity.

Avoid notification overload.

Preferred:

Few meaningful notifications.

Avoid:

Multiple daily reminders without purpose.

---

# 7. Notification Content Structure

Recommended format:

```text id="k9j3qa"
Context

↓

Value

↓

Optional action
```

Example:

```text id="p7v2lm"
Your body has been asking for extra care today.

Would you like to take a quiet moment with Lumi?

[Open Lumi]
```

---

# 8. Notification Language

Notifications should be:

* Short
* Warm
* Clear
* Human

Preferred:

"Take a moment for yourself."

Avoid:

"Complete your task now."

---

# 9. Personalization

Notifications may adapt based on:

* User preferences
* Selected goals
* Usage patterns
* Chosen routines

Personalization must:

* Respect consent
* Be transparent
* Avoid assumptions

---

# 10. AI-Powered Notifications

Lumi AI may personalize timing and content.

AI must not:

* Pressure users
* Predict sensitive information without permission
* Create fear or urgency

---

# 11. Notification Permission Experience

Permission requests should explain value.

Bad:

"Allow notifications?"

Good:

"Would you like gentle reminders for your wellness moments?"

---

# 12. Notification States

Every notification system should support:

## Permission Not Granted

Explain benefits.

---

## Enabled

Allow management.

---

## Disabled

Respect choice.

---

## Failed Delivery

Handle silently when possible.

---

# 13. Accessibility

Notifications must support:

□ Screen readers

□ Clear language

□ Adjustable settings

□ Reduced motion

□ Do not disturb preferences

---

# 14. Notification UI Components

Notification components may include:

* In-app banners
* Toast messages
* Push notifications
* Email notifications
* Reminders

Each type should have a clear purpose.

---

# 15. Notification Checklist

Before release:

□ User value is clear

□ Permission experience exists

□ Frequency is appropriate

□ Language reviewed

□ No guilt-based messaging

□ Accessibility tested

□ User controls available

---

# 16. Future Evolution

Future notification experiences may include:

* Adaptive reminders
* AI personalization
* Voice notifications
* Context-aware support

Every notification should preserve:

Trust

Calm

Autonomy
