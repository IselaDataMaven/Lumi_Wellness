# 1. Purpose

This document defines the onboarding experience for Lumi Wellness.

The onboarding process introduces users to Lumi, builds trust, and creates a personalized starting experience.

The goal is not to collect information.

The goal is to create connection.

---

# 2. Onboarding Philosophy

The first experience should feel:

🌿 Welcoming

🤍 Safe

✨ Simple

🌸 Personal

Users should think:

"I understand what Lumi is."

"I know what I can do next."

"I feel comfortable continuing."

---

# 3. Onboarding Principles

## Start with trust

Before asking questions, explain:

* Who Lumi is
* How Lumi helps
* How information is used

---

## Ask only what matters

Every question should have a purpose.

Avoid:

✘ Long questionnaires

✘ Unnecessary personal data

✘ Complex setup

---

## Give value quickly

The user should experience Lumi's value within minutes.

---

---

# 5. Screen: Welcome

## Purpose

Create the first emotional connection.

---

## Content

Example:

```text id="8l6k6j"
Welcome to Lumi 🌸

A calm space to reflect,
grow, and take care of yourself.

[Begin]
```

---

## Rules

Do:

✔ Use warm language

✔ Keep visual simplicity

✔ Show the product purpose

Avoid:

✘ Feature lists

✘ Overwhelming explanations

---

# 6. Screen: Meet Lumi

## Purpose

Introduce the AI companion.

---

## Example

```text id="hmj9vu"
I'm Lumi.

I'm here to support your wellness journey,
one small step at a time.

[Continue]
```

---

## Trust Information

Explain:

* Lumi provides support
* Users control their choices
* Privacy matters

---

# 7. Screen: Wellness Goals

## Purpose

Understand user intentions.

---

## Question

Example:

```text id="7v6jhf"
What would you like to focus on?
```

---

## Options

Examples:

🌿 Reduce stress

🌙 Improve sleep

✨ Build healthy habits

📖 Reflect more often

💛 Understand emotions

---

## Rules

Allow:

* Multiple selections
* Skipping
* Changing later

---

# 8. Screen: Personal Preferences

## Purpose

Customize experience.

---

Possible preferences:

* Preferred activities
* Reminder style
* Session duration
* Communication preferences

---

Avoid asking:

* Sensitive information without reason
* Information unrelated to experience

---

# 9. First Personalized Moment

## Purpose

Create the first value experience.

---

Examples:

Based on goals:

Stress:

↓

"Would you like to try a 2-minute breathing exercise?"

---

Reflection:

↓

"Would you like to write your first journal entry?"

---

Sleep:

↓

"Let's create a calming evening routine."

---

# 10. First AI Conversation

## Purpose

Introduce interaction with Lumi.

---

Recommended flow:

```text id="o3k2i8"
Lumi greeting

↓

User response

↓

Supportive reply

↓

Optional suggestion
```

---

Example:

```text id="e3l4om"
Lumi:

Welcome. I'm glad you're here.

How are you feeling today?
```

---

# 11. Progress Indicator

Onboarding may include progress.

Example:

```text id="u2c6wl"
Step 2 of 5
```

---

Rules:

Do:

✔ Show progress

✔ Allow going back

Avoid:

✘ Making users feel trapped

---

# 12. Skip Options

Users should maintain control.

Examples:

"Skip for now"

"Set this up later"

---

Skipping should not create punishment.

---

# 13. Privacy & Consent

Before collecting personal information:

Explain:

* What is collected
* Why it is needed
* How users control it

---

Consent must be:

✔ Clear

✔ Optional when possible

✔ Easy to change

---

# 14. Accessibility

Onboarding must support:

✔ Screen readers

✔ Keyboard navigation

✔ Large text

✔ Reduced motion

✔ Simple language

---

# 15. Common Mistakes

Avoid:

❌ Asking everything immediately

❌ Explaining every feature

❌ Creating pressure

❌ Blocking access without setup

---

# 16. Onboarding Success Metrics

Measure:

* Completion rate
* Time to first value
* First activity completion
* User confidence
* Return rate

---

# 17. Onboarding Checklist

Before release:

□ Clear introduction

□ Value shown quickly

□ Questions have purpose

□ Privacy explained

□ Skip options available

□ First experience completed

□ Accessibility tested

---

# 18. Future Evolution

Future onboarding experiences may include:

* Adaptive AI onboarding
* Voice introductions
* Personalized journeys
* Accessibility-first onboarding modes

Every improvement should preserve:

Trust

Calm

Connection
