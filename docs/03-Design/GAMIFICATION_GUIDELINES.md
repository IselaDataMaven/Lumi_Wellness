# 🌸 GAMIFICATION_GUIDELINES.md

> **Lumi Wellness Gamification Guidelines**
>
> **Version:** 1.0.0
>
> **Status:** Living Document

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines how gamification should be designed and implemented across Lumi Wellness.

The purpose of gamification is to encourage:

🌿 Consistency

🤍 Self-awareness

✨ Positive habits

🌸 Personal growth

Gamification should support wellness behaviors without creating pressure or competition.

---

# 2. Gamification Philosophy

Lumi Wellness does not gamify achievement.

Lumi celebrates progress.

The goal is not:

"Do more."

The goal is:

"Notice your journey."

---

# 3. Core Principles

## Progress over perfection

Small actions matter.

A user should feel successful even with small steps.

Example:

"Today you took a moment for yourself."

---

## Encourage, never punish

Never create feelings of failure.

Avoid:

"You broke your streak."

Prefer:

"Welcome back. Every moment counts."

---

## Personal journey over competition

Lumi should not compare users.

Avoid:

"You're behind other users."

Prefer:

"You are building your own path."

---

## Meaning over points

Rewards should represent growth.

Not just numbers.

---

# 4. Gamification Elements

Lumi may include:

## Progress Tracking

Examples:

* Wellness journey
* Personal goals
* Reflection history

Purpose:

Help users visualize growth.

---

## Milestones

Celebrate meaningful moments.

Examples:

* First journal entry
* First week of reflection
* Completing a personal goal

---

## Achievements

Achievements should represent experiences.

Examples:

🌱 First Step

Completed your first wellness activity.

---

🌸 Reflection Moment

Created your first journal entry.

---

✨ Self-Care Explorer

Tried different wellness activities.

---

# 5. Rewards Design

Rewards should feel:

✔ Meaningful

✔ Personal

✔ Encouraging

Avoid rewards that create pressure.

---

Good:

"You created space for yourself today."

Avoid:

"You failed to maintain your 30-day streak."

---

# 6. Streak Guidelines

Streaks can motivate but may create anxiety.

If streaks are used:

Always provide:

* Flexible recovery
* Positive language
* No punishment

---

Good:

"Your journey continues."

Avoid:

"You lost your progress."

---

# 7. Notifications

Gamification notifications should be supportive.

Good:

"Your wellness moment is waiting whenever you are ready."

Avoid:

"Don't break your streak!"

---

# 8. Progress Visualization

Progress should communicate:

* Growth
* Reflection
* Encouragement

Avoid:

* Excessive numbers
* Competitive rankings
* Pressure indicators

---

# 9. User Control

Users should control:

* Notifications
* Visibility of achievements
* Gamification preferences

---

# 10. Accessibility

Gamification must support:

□ Screen readers

□ Color-independent feedback

□ Clear descriptions

□ Reduced motion

□ Alternative progress indicators

---

# 11. Emotional Safety

Gamification should never:

✘ Shame users

✘ Create anxiety

✘ Encourage unhealthy behavior

✘ Replace personal motivation

---

# 12. AI and Gamification

Lumi AI may help celebrate progress.

Example:

"You have been taking time to reflect more often. That's meaningful progress."

AI should not:

* Pressure users
* Compare performance
* Create artificial urgency

---

# 13. Visual Guidelines

Gamification visuals should feel:

🌿 Calm

🌸 Warm

✨ Rewarding

Avoid:

* Aggressive badges
* Competitive visuals
* Excessive celebrations

---

# 14. Gamification States

Components should define:

## Locked

Future achievement.

## Available

Ready to unlock.

## Completed

Achievement reached.

## In Progress

Journey ongoing.

---

# 15. Checklist

Before releasing gamification features:

□ Supports healthy behavior

□ Does not create guilt

□ User controls notifications

□ Accessibility reviewed

□ Language matches Lumi voice

□ Rewards have meaning

□ No unhealthy competition

---

# 16. Future Evolution

Future gamification may include:

* Personalized journeys
* AI-guided reflections
* Gentle challenges
* Community experiences

Every feature should preserve:

Compassion

Autonomy

Well-being
