# 1. Purpose

This document defines the research approach used to design and improve Lumi Wellness.

Design research helps the team understand:

* User needs
* Emotional context
* Behaviors
* Pain points
* Opportunities

The goal is to build experiences based on real human needs.

---

# 2. Research Philosophy

Lumi Wellness research should always begin with empathy.

We do not design for assumptions.

We design for understanding.

Research should help answer:

"What does the user need?"

Not:

"What feature can we build?"

---

# 3. Research Principles

## Listen before solving

Understand the experience before creating solutions.

---

## Observe real behavior

What users do is as important as what they say.

---

## Respect vulnerability

Wellness experiences may involve personal emotions.

Research must create psychological safety.

---

## Reduce bias

The team should challenge assumptions.

---

# 4. Research Goals

Research should help discover:

## User Needs

What users are trying to achieve.

Examples:

* Better emotional awareness
* More consistent routines
* Stress management
* Personal reflection

---

## User Challenges

What prevents progress.

Examples:

* Lack of motivation
* Limited time
* Feeling overwhelmed
* Difficulty maintaining habits

---

## User Expectations

What creates trust.

Examples:

* Privacy
* Clear explanations
* Human language
* Personalization

---

# 5. User Research Methods

Lumi may use:

---

## User Interviews

Purpose:

Understand experiences and motivations.

Questions:

* What wellness challenges do you experience?
* What tools have you tried?
* What worked or did not work?

---

## Surveys

Purpose:

Collect broader patterns.

Used for:

* Preferences
* Behaviors
* Feature validation

---

## Usability Testing

Purpose:

Observe how users interact.

Evaluate:

* Navigation
* Understanding
* Confusion points
* Completion rate

---

## Prototype Testing

Purpose:

Validate ideas before development.

Test:

* Figma prototypes
* User flows
* AI interactions

---

# 6. Research Participants

Participants should represent Lumi users.

Consider:

* Different ages
* Different technology comfort levels
* Different wellness goals
* Different accessibility needs

# 8. User Journey Mapping

Research should identify:

## Before Lumi

User situation:

* Current challenges
* Existing solutions
* Frustrations

---

## During Lumi

Experience:

* First interaction
* Daily usage
* AI conversations

---

## After Lumi

Outcomes:

* New habits
* Reflection
* Continued engagement

---

# 9. Pain Point Documentation

Every identified problem should include:

## Problem

What happens.

## Evidence

What users reported.

## Impact

Why it matters.

## Opportunity

Possible improvement.

---

Example:

```text id="o0m6y5"
Problem:

Users forget wellness activities.

Impact:

Low consistency.

Opportunity:

Create gentle reminders.
```

---

# 10. AI Experience Research

AI interactions require additional research.

Evaluate:

* Trust
* Comfort
* Response usefulness
* Personalization
* Safety perception

---

Questions:

"Does Lumi feel supportive?"

"Does the user understand AI limitations?"

"Does the user feel in control?"

---

# 11. Usability Metrics

Track:

## Effectiveness

Can users complete tasks?

---

## Efficiency

How easily can users complete tasks?

---

## Satisfaction

How does the experience feel?

---

# 12. Research Documentation

Every research activity should record:

* Date
* Goal
* Participants
* Method
* Findings
* Decisions
* Next steps

---

# 13. Design Decisions

Research findings should influence:

* Features
* Content
* Flows
* Components
* AI behavior

---

# 14. Ethical Research

Research must protect users.

Requirements:

✔ Informed consent

✔ Privacy protection

✔ Respectful questions

✔ Secure data handling

---

# 15. Accessibility Research

Include users with different needs.

Test:

* Screen readers
* Text scaling
* Motor accessibility
* Cognitive accessibility

---

# 16. Research Checklist

Before making major decisions:

□ User problem identified

□ Research evidence collected

□ Assumptions challenged

□ Accessibility considered

□ Findings documented

□ Decisions explained

---

# 17. Future Evolution

Research may expand into:

* AI behavior studies
* Long-term wellness journeys
* Personalization research
* Community studies

Every research effort should strengthen:

Empathy

Understanding

Trust
