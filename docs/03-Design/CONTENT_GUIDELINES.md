# 1. Purpose

This document defines the voice, tone, and writing principles for Lumi Wellness.

The goal is to create communication that feels:

🌿 Supportive

🤍 Human

✨ Clear

🌸 Encouraging

Every message should help users feel understood and confident.

---

# 2. Lumi Voice

Lumi speaks like a supportive companion.

The voice is:

## Warm

Lumi creates connection.

Example:

"Welcome back. It's good to see you."

---

## Calm

Lumi never creates urgency unnecessarily.

Example:

"Take your time. You can continue whenever you're ready."

---

## Encouraging

Lumi recognizes effort.

Example:

"Small steps are meaningful progress."

---

## Respectful

Lumi respects personal choices.

Example:

"Would you like to try a different activity?"

---

# 3. Lumi Is Not

Lumi should never sound:

❌ Clinical

"You are experiencing emotional dysregulation."

Prefer:

"Your feelings are valid. Let's explore what you're experiencing."

---

❌ Judgmental

"You failed your daily goal."

Prefer:

"Your journey continues whenever you are ready."

---

❌ Robotic

"Your wellness score has decreased."

Prefer:

"Your recent check-ins show that you may need extra care today."

---

❌ Overly Cheerful

Avoid forced positivity.

Do not say:

"Everything is amazing!"

when a user expresses difficulty.

---

# 4. Tone Principles

## Meet the user where they are.

The tone adapts to the user's emotional context.

---

## Positive does not mean ignoring difficulty.

Validate first.

Example:

"That sounds like a difficult moment.

Would you like to try a short breathing exercise?"

---

# 5. Writing Style

Use:

✔ Short sentences

✔ Simple vocabulary

✔ Active voice

✔ Friendly language

✔ Clear instructions

---

Avoid:

✘ Long paragraphs

✘ Technical jargon

✘ Corporate language

✘ Excessive emojis

---

# 6. Buttons & Actions

Buttons should describe the action.

Good:

✔ Start Reflection

✔ Save Journal

✔ Continue

✔ Try Breathing Exercise

---

Avoid:

❌ Submit

❌ Process

❌ Execute

---

# 7. Empty State Messages

Empty states should guide, not feel incomplete.

Structure:

1. Acknowledge

2. Explain

3. Offer next step

Example:

```
🌱 Your wellness journey starts here.

Create your first goal and take a small step forward.

[Create Goal]
```

---

# 8. Error Messages

Errors should be:

* Clear
* Helpful
* Non-blaming

Structure:

What happened

*

What to do next

---

Bad:

"Error 503."

---

Good:

"We couldn't connect right now.

Please try again in a moment."

---

# 9. Success Messages

Celebrate without creating pressure.

Good:

"Your reflection has been saved 🌿"

"Nice work taking a moment for yourself."

---

Avoid:

"You are crushing your goals!"

---

# 10. Notifications

Notifications should feel like invitations.

Good:

"Take a moment to check in with yourself today 🌸"

"Your journal is waiting whenever you feel ready."

---

Avoid:

"You haven't completed your activity."

---

# 11. AI Companion Writing

Lumi AI should:

## Acknowledge

"I hear you."

---

## Support

"Let's explore this together."

---

## Suggest

"Would you like to try a short exercise?"

---

## Respect

"You decide what feels right for you."

---

# 12. Mental Health Safety Language

Lumi should:

* Encourage seeking professional support when appropriate
* Avoid diagnosis
* Avoid medical claims

---

Avoid:

"You have anxiety."

Prefer:

"Some of these feelings can be difficult to manage."

---

# 13. Emoji Guidelines

Emojis may be used sparingly.

Good:

🌿 Progress

✨ Encouragement

🌸 Calm moments

Avoid:

* Excessive emojis
* Emojis replacing words
* Emojis in serious messages

---

# 14. Localization

Content should support:

* Spanish
* English
* Future languages

Avoid:

* Culture-specific expressions that cannot translate
* Complex wordplay

---

# 15. Accessibility Writing

Content must be:

* Easy to understand
* Screen-reader friendly
* Free of unnecessary abbreviations

---

# 16. Content Checklist

Before publishing any text:

□ Is it clear?

□ Is it supportive?

□ Does it respect user autonomy?

□ Is it accessible?

□ Does it sound human?

□ Does it avoid judgment?

---

# 17. Future Evolution

Lumi's language may evolve with:

* User feedback
* Cultural adaptation
* AI improvements
* New wellness experiences

The voice must always preserve:

Kindness

Clarity

Trust
