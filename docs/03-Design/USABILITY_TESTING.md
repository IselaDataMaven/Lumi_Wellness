# 1. Purpose

This document defines the usability testing process for Lumi Wellness.

Usability testing helps validate that users can:

* Understand the product
* Complete important tasks
* Navigate confidently
* Feel comfortable using Lumi

---

# 2. Testing Philosophy

The goal is not to test users.

The goal is to test the experience.

Users are never wrong.

Confusion is a signal that the design can improve.

---

# 3. Usability Goals

Testing should evaluate:

## Learnability

Can a new user understand Lumi?

---

## Efficiency

Can users complete tasks easily?

---

## Effectiveness

Can users achieve their goals?

---

## Satisfaction

Does the experience feel comfortable and valuable?

---

## Trust

Does the user feel safe interacting with Lumi AI?

---

# 4. Testing Methods

## Moderated Testing

A researcher observes users completing tasks.

Useful for:

* Early prototypes
* Complex flows

---

## Unmoderated Testing

Users complete tasks independently.

Useful for:

* Larger samples
* Quantitative insights

---

## Prototype Testing

Used before development.

Test:

* Figma prototypes
* User flows
* AI conversations

---

## Production Testing

Used after launch.

Evaluate:

* Real behavior
* Drop-off points
* Feature adoption

---

# 5. Participant Selection

Participants should represent Lumi users.

Consider:

* Different ages
* Different technology experience
* Different wellness goals
* Accessibility needs

---

# 6. Testing Preparation

Every test should define:

## Objective

What we want to learn.

---

## Participants

Who is testing.

---

## Scenario

The situation presented.

---

## Tasks

What users attempt.

---

## Success Criteria

How completion is measured.

---

# 7. Test Scenarios

## Scenario: First Experience

Goal:

Understand onboarding.

Task:

"Create your account and begin your first Lumi experience."

Observe:

* Confusion points
* Questions
* Completion time

---

## Scenario: Emotional Check-In

Goal:

Understand mood tracking.

Task:

"Tell Lumi how you are feeling today."

Observe:

* Comfort level
* Understanding
* Emotional safety

---

## Scenario: AI Conversation

Goal:

Evaluate AI interaction.

Task:

"Talk with Lumi about a difficult day."

Observe:

* Trust
* Response satisfaction
* Clarity

---

## Scenario: Journal Entry

Goal:

Evaluate reflection experience.

Task:

"Write and save a personal reflection."

Observe:

* Privacy perception
* Ease of use

---

# 8. Task Measurement

Track:

## Completion Rate

Did users finish?

---

## Time on Task

How long did it take?

---

## Errors

Where did users struggle?

---

## Assistance Needed

Did users require help?

---

## Satisfaction Score

How did users feel?

---

# 9. Qualitative Feedback

Collect:

* User comments
* Emotional reactions
* Suggestions
* Confusion points

---

Important questions:

"What did you expect to happen?"

"What felt unclear?"

"How did this experience make you feel?"

---

# 10. AI-Specific Testing

Evaluate:

## Trust

Does Lumi feel reliable?

---

## Tone

Does Lumi feel supportive?

---

## Boundaries

Does Lumi respect user choice?

---

## Safety

Does Lumi handle sensitive situations appropriately?

---

# 11. Accessibility Testing

Include:

* Keyboard navigation
* Screen readers
* Text scaling
* Contrast
* Reduced motion

---

# 12. Severity Classification

Issues should be categorized.

## Critical

Blocks user completion.

Example:

Cannot create account.

---

## High

Creates major confusion.

Example:

Cannot find journal.

---

## Medium

Creates friction.

Example:

Button placement unclear.

---

## Low

Minor improvement.

Example:

Spacing inconsistency.
