> **Lumi Wellness Privacy UX Guidelines**
>
> **Version:** 1.0.0
>
> **Status:** Living Document

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines how privacy should be designed and communicated across Lumi Wellness.

The goal is to create experiences where users understand:

* What information is collected
* Why it is needed
* How it is used
* How they maintain control

Privacy should feel:

🌿 Transparent

🤍 Respectful

✨ Simple

🌸 Trustworthy

---

# 2. Privacy Philosophy

Privacy is part of the user experience.

Users should never feel:

"I don't know what happens with my information."

They should feel:

"I understand and control my choices."

---

# 3. Privacy Principles

## Transparency First

Explain information usage clearly.

Avoid:

* Complex legal language
* Hidden settings
* Confusing permissions

---

## User Control

Users should control:

* What they share
* What is remembered
* What notifications they receive
* What data they delete

---

## Ask Only When Needed

Collect only information that improves the experience.

Every data request should have a purpose.

---

## Respect Sensitive Moments

Wellness conversations may be personal.

Design should prioritize:

* Trust
* Comfort
* Consent

---

# 4. Privacy Communication

Privacy messages should be:

* Short
* Clear
* Human

Avoid:

"Your data will be processed according to applicable regulations."

Prefer:

"Your reflections belong to you. You choose how Lumi uses them."

---

# 5. Consent Experience

Consent should be:

## Informed

Users understand what they accept.

---

## Specific

Each permission has a clear purpose.

---

## Optional When Possible

Users can continue without unnecessary sharing.

---

# 6. Permission Requests

Permission requests should explain value.

Bad:

"Allow access to your data."

Good:

"Would you like Lumi to remember your preferences so your experience feels more personalized?"

---

# 7. AI Privacy

For Lumi AI experiences:

Users should understand:

* When they are interacting with AI
* How conversations are handled
* Whether memory is enabled
* How personalization works

---

# 8. AI Memory Controls

If memory features exist, users should have:

## Visibility

See what Lumi remembers.

---

## Control

Choose what is stored.

---

## Removal

Delete remembered information.

---

Example:

"Lumi remembers that you prefer short evening reflections."

[Keep]

[Remove]

---

# 9. Data Sharing

Users should understand:

* Whether data is shared
* Why sharing occurs
* How to manage preferences

Avoid:

Hidden sharing practices.

---

# 10. Privacy Settings

Privacy settings should be:

* Easy to find
* Easy to understand
* Grouped logically

Possible sections:

```text id="q1h8sv"
Account Privacy

↓

AI Memory

↓

Notifications

↓

Data Management
```

---

# 11. Data Deletion Experience

Deleting data should be clear.

Users should know:

* What will be deleted
* What cannot be recovered
* How long the process takes

Avoid:

Making deletion intentionally difficult.

---

# 12. Privacy States

Every privacy feature should support:

## Not Configured

Explain available choices.

---

## Enabled

Show active permissions.

---

## Disabled

Respect the user's decision.

---

## Changed

Confirm updates.

---

# 13. Privacy and Forms

When collecting personal information:

Include:

* Purpose explanation
* Optional indicators
* Clear consent

Avoid:

Collecting information without context.

---

# 14. Privacy and Notifications

Notifications should avoid exposing sensitive information.

Bad:

"Your pain journal shows increased symptoms."

Good:

"Your wellness reflection is ready."

---

# 15. Accessibility

Privacy experiences must support:

□ Clear language

□ Screen readers

□ Keyboard navigation

□ Understandable controls

□ Accessible consent flows

---

# 16. Privacy Checklist

Before release:

□ Data purpose is explained

□ Consent is clear

□ Users have control

□ AI behavior is transparent

□ Sensitive information is protected

□ Deletion options exist

□ Accessibility reviewed

---

# 17. Future Evolution

Privacy UX may evolve with:

* Advanced AI personalization
* New privacy regulations
* User-controlled data models
* More transparent AI systems

Every privacy decision should preserve:

Trust

Respect

User autonomy
