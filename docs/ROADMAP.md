# 1. Purpose

This document defines the product evolution strategy for Lumi Wellness.

The roadmap helps align:

* Product vision
* User needs
* Development priorities
* Design decisions
* Technical investments

---

# 2. Product Vision

Lumi Wellness exists to support people living with chronic pain through a compassionate, accessible, and human-centered digital experience.

Our goal is to create a wellness companion that helps users:

🌿 Understand their daily experience

🤍 Build healthy habits

✨ Feel supported

🌸 Take care of themselves without judgment

---

# 3. Roadmap Principles

Every feature should answer:

> Does this improve the user's wellbeing experience?

Priorities:

* User impact over feature quantity
* Simplicity over complexity
* Accessibility by default
* Privacy by design
* Sustainable growth

---

* Personalized welcome experience

* Mood tracking

* Entry history

## Interface

* Responsive experience

* Accessible components

# Phase 2 — Personalization

## Complexity
